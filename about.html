About Trupti Yoga Studio

A place to learn, practice, and grow — calmly, patiently, and at your own pace.

Your teacher and guide

Our Story

Trupti Yoga Studio began with a simple desire — to share the benefits of yoga with our community and create a welcoming space where people can learn and practice with comfort and confidence.

Yoga is for everyone. You do not need to be flexible, experienced, or physically strong to begin. Our classes are conducted with care and attention to individual needs, in a safe and supportive environment, without pressure.

About A. Nithiyanandham

I am a dedicated yoga teacher and Noble World Record Director (Yoga), with qualifications including M.Sc. (Yoga), M.Com, M.Acu., and International Yoga Teacher Training. I have participated in and received recognition at state, national, and international-level yoga competitions, and have been honoured with the Yoga Ratna, Best Teacher, Best Yoga Center, and Thirumoolar Awards.

The most rewarding part of teaching is seeing a student become healthier, more confident, calmer, and more aware through regular practice. My role is to guide each student patiently as they progress at their own pace.
